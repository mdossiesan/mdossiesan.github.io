How it works

Three steps. Every day.

Tracking only works if it's quick. DSJ keeps it to three moves you can make in under a minute.

  1. 1

    Log it

    Snap a meal, scan a label or type it in. Add your run with distance and time.

  2. 2

    See it

    Your day adds up as you go: calories left, protein, carbs, fat and weekly miles.

  3. 3

    Shape it

    Set your own targets, adjust them as you learn, and keep notes on what works.

Questions

Good to know

What is DSJ?

DSJ is the personal site of Dos Santos Jesus and the home of a tracking system for food, runs and notes.

What can I track right now?

The pages are being built step by step. Start Tracking and Personal Notes are already in the menu, and the build log above shows what comes next.

Will it scan food with the camera?

That's the plan: snap a meal or a nutrition label, or scan a barcode, and get calories, protein, carbs and fat that you can adjust before saving.

Does the site store anything about me?

Not yet. The site doesn't collect or save anything right now.

Does it cost anything?

No. The site is free to visit and is hosted on GitHub Pages.
